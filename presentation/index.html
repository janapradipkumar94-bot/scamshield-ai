Introduction & Problem Statement
Understanding the rapid proliferation of digital fraud across India's expanding digital ecosystem.
JIS COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING
The Threat Landscape: 13+ Billion Monthly Digital Payments with Escalating Social Engineering
With India processing over 13+ billion monthly UPI transactions, malicious threat actors increasingly target everyday citizens rather than bank mainframes. Through psychological manipulation, fake authority threats, and typosquatted lookalike domains, billions of rupees are lost before victims realise they have been compromised.
ATTACK VECTOR 1
Phishing & Lookalike URLs
Attackers register deceptive top-level domains (e.g. sbi-kyc-verify.xyz, bill-update.top) that trick victims into entering debit card numbers, passwords, and UPI PINs on forged portal layouts.
ATTACK VECTOR 2
Urgency & Fear Coercion
Fraud messages leverage psychological pressure: immediate power cut at 9:30 PM, YONO account suspension within 12 hours, or "Digital Arrest" warrants, inducing panic and clouding rational scrutiny.
ACCESSIBILITY GAP
Language & Technical Hurdle
Over 70% of new digital consumers in Tier-2/3 regions and senior citizens do not speak English fluently and cannot inspect raw SSL certificates or URL syntax, making accessible native-language tools vital.

System Architecture & Working Process
End-to-end processing pipeline from raw user input to actionable security intelligence.
JIS COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING
STEP 01
User Input
User pastes SMS, WhatsApp forward, suspicious link, or email in web app.
STEP 02
Preprocessing
Lexical parsing, IP & TLD extraction, token normalization, n-gram generation.
STEP 03
AI Analysis
TF-IDF vector mapping against trained vocabulary + domain heuristic weights.
STEP 04
Scam Detection
Probability calculation, 0-100 score assignment, and trigger token extraction.
STEP 05
Result Display
Risk gauge, plain-English advice, vernacular TTS voice, and 1930 helpline dialer.
LEXICAL HEURISTIC ENGINE
Deterministic URL Analysis
Inspects Top-Level Domains (.xyz, .top, .work), IP address hostings, typosquatted brand names (e.g. sbi, yono, netflix), and suspicious port bindings without navigating to the dangerous link.
STATISTICAL NLP CLASSIFIER
TF-IDF + Logistic Regression
Trained on real Indian cyber threat samples. Maps n-gram frequency distributions to detect urgency, lottery winnings, power cut coercion, and fraudulent credential requests.
PERFORMANCE & SCALABILITY
Sub-50ms Inference Latency
Lightweight serialized model (Joblib) loads into memory on startup. FastAPI asynchronous workers process threat evaluations in under 50 milliseconds with high throughput.
